# Brand Guidelines — Northbrook Outfitters

Northbrook Outfitters is a fictional mid-size outdoor apparel and gear
retailer, sold direct-to-consumer online and through 40 physical stores
in the northeastern United States, used here as a demo brand.

## Brand Voice

Northbrook's voice is **practical, warm, and unpretentious**. We speak
like an experienced hiking friend, not a luxury outdoor brand. Avoid
extreme-sport bravado ("conquer," "dominate," "crush the trail") and
avoid overly technical gear-jargon unless the audience is our
"Trail Veterans" segment (see customer_segments.md).

Tone guidelines:
- Use second person ("you") and active voice.
- Favor concrete, sensory language ("the kind of jacket that shrugs off
  a surprise downpour") over vague superlatives ("the best jacket ever").
- Humor is allowed but should be gentle, never sarcastic toward the
  customer's skill level or gear choices.
- Never use fear-based urgency ("last chance," "don't miss out or
  regret it") — this conflicts with our brand promise of low-pressure,
  trustworthy advice.

## Visual Identity

- Primary palette: forest green (#2F4F3D), warm sand (#D8C7A1), and
  charcoal (#2B2B2B).
- Typography: a humanist sans-serif for body copy, a slab serif for
  headlines evoking trail signage.
- Photography should show real terrain and real weather conditions —
  no obviously staged studio shots for outdoor product lines.

## Messaging Pillars

1. **Gear that earns trust over time** — durability and repairability
   messaging is core to the brand, not an afterthought.
2. **Every skill level welcome** — campaigns should not make beginners
   feel unqualified to enjoy the outdoors.
3. **Repair over replace** — Northbrook offers a lifetime repair
   program; this should be referenced in loyalty and retention
   campaigns where relevant.

## Prohibited Claims

Marketing copy must NOT:
- State or imply specific environmental impact numbers (e.g. carbon
  offset amounts) unless sourced from the current verified
  sustainability report — copywriters do not have authority to
  generate these figures.
- Compare Northbrook directly to named competitor brands by name in
  paid advertising.
- Promise specific weather performance outcomes ("guaranteed dry in
  any storm") — use qualified language ("designed to handle heavy
  rain") instead.

## Approval Requirements

Any campaign referencing a specific discount percentage, a specific
delivery-time promise, or a specific inventory/stock claim must be
checked against current operations data before publishing — Marketing
does not have standing authority to state these as fact from brand
guidelines alone.
